Global Data Model

The Global Data Model is a browser-readable schema for online IWT monitoring. It organizes observation metadata, taxon and product evidence, trade signals, actor and network context, platform and location cues, and governance fields into one exportable catalogue.

How it was curated

    Platform assessment criteria

      RSBA and RSBR

      The requested RSBA concept is represented here as a practical rapid baseline workflow: use the field catalogue to scope data collection, the RSBR table to prioritize species baskets, and the platform ranking to decide where monitoring capacity should be focused first.
