Priority species and products

In cases where resources are limited, it is best to establish a shortlist of priority species or product types. Priority-setting should balance conservation urgency, enforcement relevance, and the practical likelihood that the species or product can be identified and assessed online with enough confidence to make monitoring useful.

A simple weighted scoring system across these criteria helps analysts rank species or product groups during initial scoping for the ECOSOLVE Global Monitoring System (GMS), which uses open-source intelligence (OSINT) and artificial intelligence (AI) to track, analyze, and disrupt illicit wildlife markets on social media and e-commerce platforms.

    Global Data Model

    The Global Data Model is a structured schema for online IWT monitoring. It organizes observation metadata, taxon and product evidence, trade signals, actor and network context, platform and location cues, and governance fields into one exportable catalogue.

    How to use

      RSBA workflow

      The Rapid Species Basket Assessment turns species-list evidence into Retain, Monitor, or Drop recommendations. Edit criteria values directly in the table, then run scoring again.

      Platform assessment workflow

      Platform rows can be entered manually, loaded from the example, or uploaded with the template columns. Scores are summed and ranked as High priority, Monitor, or Low priority.

      Developed in cooperation with the Data Interoperability Working Group under the ECOSOLVE project by Global Initiative Against Transnational Organized Crime
